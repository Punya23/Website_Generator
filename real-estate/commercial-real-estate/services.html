FAQ

Common questions

Most Chicagoland office and retail leases are structured as full-service gross or modified gross, while industrial space is typically triple net (NNN). We'll walk through the difference and model total occupancy cost for any structure during your consultation.

In most leasing transactions, commission is paid by the landlord as part of the deal, so tenant representation carries no direct cost to you. Investment sales commissions are negotiated per engagement and disclosed up front — there are never hidden fees.

A straightforward office or retail lease typically takes 60-90 days from site selection to occupancy. Investment sales usually run 90-150 days depending on financing and due diligence complexity.

We cover the Loop, West Loop, River North, and greater Chicagoland, including the O'Hare industrial corridor, Schaumburg, Naperville, and the surrounding collar suburbs.

We typically represent investment sales starting around $1.5 million, with our team most active in the $3 million to $25 million range. Reach out regardless of size — we can point you in the right direction even outside that band.
