# Skin design references

Skins in this catalog are **original compositions** of our React section library. They are not copies of third-party HTML, CSS, or Framer source.

## Allowed references

MIT (or similarly permissive) marketing templates, used as composition recipes — hero type, section order, density — then re-authored:

- [HyperUI](https://github.com/markmead/hyperui) (MIT) — centered type heroes and banners
- [Start Bootstrap landing page](https://github.com/StartBootstrap/startbootstrap-landing-page) (MIT) — type masthead → numbered features → flipping image/copy showcases → testimonials → CTA
- [TailGrids Play](https://github.com/TailGrids/play-tailwind) (MIT)
- AstroWind / Astroship (MIT)
- shadcn-style landing starters (MIT)

Each skin's `inspiredBy` field names the recipe. Homepage DNA is deliberately not "hero → stats → cards → testimonial → CTA" on every skin. About pages open on `story_split`, not a cloned intro + team grid.

## Framer

Public Framer Community pages may be **screenshotted into a private moodboard** for art direction only. Do not scrape, vendor, or ship Framer HTML/CSS/JS. Do not pixel-clone a distinctive template.

Put local screenshots in this folder if needed; they must stay out of generated sites and out of git if they are not ours.

## Do not use as inventory

Tailwind Plus, Cruip paid kits, ThemeForest, Webflow cloneables, and Framer source.
