About Adriana

Adriana founded Aurelia Estates in 2014 after a decade as a private art advisor, where she learned to navigate discretion, provenance, and value for collectors who never wanted their names in print. She brought the same philosophy to real estate: represent the property, protect the client, and let the results speak. Adriana specializes in Bel Air, Holmby Hills, and Beverly Hills, and has personally closed over $1.1 billion in transactions.

She currently sits on the board of a Los Angeles arts conservancy and continues to personally advise a small number of long-standing clients and their families.
