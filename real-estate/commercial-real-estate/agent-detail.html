About Daniel

Daniel founded Cornerstone Commercial Realty in 2008 after eight years structuring capital markets transactions at a national brokerage firm. He leads the firm's investment sales practice, focusing on office and mixed-use asset dispositions and acquisitions across Chicago and the collar suburbs, and has personally closed more than 240 transactions representing over $1.1 billion in volume. Daniel is known for underwriting that holds up under scrutiny and for negotiating from a position built entirely on data.

Outside the office, Daniel serves on the advisory board of a Chicago small-business lending nonprofit and mentors brokers early in their careers.
