Description

Located minutes from O'Hare International Airport and I-90 in the heart of the Elk Grove Village industrial corridor, this 84,000-square-foot distribution facility offers 32-foot clear heights, ESFR sprinklers, and heavy power for logistics, light manufacturing, or last-mile distribution use. The building includes 4,200 square feet of built-out office space with a conference room and private offices, and the fully fenced, secured yard accommodates trailer parking and container storage. Zoned I-1 Limited Industrial, the property is available for direct lease with occupancy available within 60 days.

Features & Specifications

  • 8 dock-high loading doors (9' x 10')
  • 1 drive-in door (12' x 14')
  • 32' clear ceiling height
  • ESFR sprinkler system
  • 3-phase, 2,000-amp power
  • Fiber connectivity (AT&T & Comcast)
  • 4,200 SF built-out office with conference room
  • Secured, fenced yard with trailer parking
