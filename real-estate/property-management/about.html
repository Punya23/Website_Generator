About Harborview Property Management

Our Story

Property management that works for both sides of the lease

Harborview Property Management was founded in 2015 by Dana Whitfield, a longtime Seattle leasing agent who kept watching owners get burned by unresponsive management companies and tenants get stuck waiting weeks for repairs. She started Harborview with a simple premise: property management should work well for both sides of the lease.

Eleven years later, Harborview manages more than 410 rental units across Seattle, the Eastside, and surrounding neighborhoods — everything from single-family homes to small multifamily buildings. Our team of licensed property managers, leasing specialists, and maintenance coordinators handles the daily work of ownership, from marketing vacancies to answering a 2am pipe leak.

We're still locally owned and Seattle-based, with every property manager living in the neighborhoods we serve. Owners get a partner who treats their investment like it matters; tenants get a management company that actually returns their calls.
