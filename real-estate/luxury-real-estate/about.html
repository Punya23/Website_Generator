About Aurelia Estates

Our Story

A brokerage built on discretion and design

Aurelia Estates was founded in 2014 by Adriana Voss, a former private art advisor who spent a decade helping collectors navigate discretion, provenance, and value — skills she found were just as essential in real estate. She started the firm after watching too many exceptional Los Angeles properties change hands through impersonal, transactional processes that ignored what her clients actually cared about: privacy, craftsmanship, and trust.

Twelve years later, Aurelia Estates represents some of the most significant addresses in Bel Air, Holmby Hills, and Beverly Hills, and along the Malibu coastline, with select advisors extending our reach into Aspen and the Hamptons. Every advisor at the firm has closed nine-figure transactions and understands that a $10 million sale demands the same care as a $50 million one.

We remain independently owned and deliberately small. Six advisors, one standard: represent every client as though the transaction were our own.
