Description

Set behind private gates on one of Bel Air's most coveted streets, this newly built compound pairs museum-quality architecture with effortless indoor-outdoor living. Walls of pocketing glass open the main living pavilion to a motor-court auto gallery, a 70-foot infinity-edge pool, and unobstructed canyon views. The primary suite occupies its own wing, with a private terrace, dual dressing rooms, and a spa bath finished in book-matched marble. A subterranean level houses a screening room, wine cellar, and wellness center with sauna and cold plunge.
