About Sarah

Sarah founded Prestige Realty in 2011 after a decade working in commercial real estate finance. A third-generation San Franciscan, she specializes in the Peninsula market — Palo Alto, Menlo Park, and Atherton — and has personally closed over 400 transactions. Sarah is known for her calm, data-driven negotiation style and her insistence that every client understand exactly what they're signing.

Outside the office, Sarah serves on the board of a Peninsula housing-access nonprofit and mentors new agents entering the brokerage.
