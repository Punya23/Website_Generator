About Cornerstone Commercial Realty

Our Story

Built on underwriting discipline, not sentiment

Cornerstone Commercial Realty was founded in 2008 by Daniel Marsh, a capital markets broker who launched the firm in the middle of a financial crisis on one conviction: commercial real estate decisions should be driven by data and comparable transactions, not by sentiment or relationships alone. That discipline shaped how the firm was built from day one.

Eighteen years later, our team of brokers has represented more than 310 properties and transacted over 42 million square feet of office, retail, and industrial space across Chicago and the collar suburbs. We remain independently owned, with every broker specializing in a specific submarket rather than covering the entire region at once.

Today, Cornerstone is known for rigorous market analysis, straightforward fee structures, and a team that stays engaged with clients well past lease signing or closing day.
