About Dana

Dana founded Harborview Property Management in 2015 after eight years managing residential portfolios for a large regional brokerage, where she grew frustrated watching owners get generic service and tenants get ignored. She built Harborview around a simple standard: every property gets a dedicated manager who actually knows it. Dana holds a Washington real estate broker's license and personally oversees the firm's largest owner accounts.

Outside the office, Dana serves on the board of a Seattle affordable-housing nonprofit and mentors new property managers joining the firm.
